答對率 43%：當年每 100 位考生，有 43 位答對。
Over the course of the morning, the innovative discussion process 14 a new form of collective effort that transformed the depth, scope, and quality of their discussion.
(A)made up for
(B)kept track of
(C)gave rise to
(D)looked out for
答案：(C) gave rise to
題目編號：G114_14_1（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題
解析
【句型】give rise to 指「引發/產生」，表示這個創新的討論過程「催生了」一種新形式的集體協作；made up for（彌補）、kept track of（追蹤）、looked out for（留意）都不合。
深入解析
你可能會這樣想
give 你最熟的意思是「給」，所以 give rise to 看起來像「給……上升」，很難直接連到「引發」。這裡的 rise 是名詞，指「產生、興起」，give rise to sth 要整組當一個字記：造成、引發，後面多半接抽象的結果，例如問題、爭議、新的事物。
怎麼判斷
句子是 the innovative discussion process ___ a new form of collective effort：主詞是「討論過程」，受詞是「一種新形式的集體協作」——一個過程「產生出」一個新東西，這是明確的因果關係。下一句 The World Café was {15} created.（世界咖啡館誕生了）回頭確認了前面講的正是「造成某物出現」。
誘答選項
made up for 是「彌補（損失、不足）」，後面要接被彌補的缺憾；kept track of 是「持續掌握（進度、動向）」；looked out for 是「留意、當心」。這三個的主詞通常是人，本句主詞是抽象的 process，一看就該起疑。
常考字詞與句型
give rise to sth（引發、造成……）、make up for sth（彌補……）、keep track of sth（掌握……的動向）、lose track of sth（跟丟、失去……的蹤影或動向）、look out for sb（留意、當心某人）、collective effort（集體協作）。

In 1995 business and academic leaders met at the Mill Valley home of Juanita Brown and David Isaacs. They did not know they were inventing something new.
Heavy rain ruined their plan for a big circle discussion in the garden. Two dozen people crowded indoors and talked in small groups at tables.
They changed tables from time to time, so ideas travelled and deepened. That morning produced the World Café, now used by businesses and schools worldwide.
