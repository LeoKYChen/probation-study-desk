連接詞／轉折詞線索：跨句
They are naturally equipped with 16 they need to groom themselves: a flexible tongue with which to lick, forepaws they moisten with saliva and use like washcloths, and teeth to dig out tougher pieces of food. 17, adult cats may spend as much as half of their waking hours grooming themselves, their relatives and friends.
(A)In a nutshell
(B)Believe it or not
(C)On the other hand
(D)To make matters worse
答案：(B) Believe it or not
題目編號：GRef_17_1（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題
解析
Believe it or not（信不信由你）符合這種帶出令人意外的事實（成貓花一半清醒時間理毛）的語氣；In a nutshell（簡言之）、On the other hand（另一方面）、To make matters worse（更糟的是）都不合。
深入解析
本題大考中心未公布逐題答對率，以下不談數據，只談觀念。
你可能會這樣想
四個選項都是句首的轉折詞，而這一段一直在講貓有多愛乾淨，To make matters worse（更糟的是）看起來像要接一個負面的轉折。這一格要看的是後面那個數字給人的感覺。
怎麼判斷
四個選項都是轉折詞，可見這一格考的是這一句的語氣。先看後面接什麼：adult cats may spend as much as half of their waking hours grooming themselves, their relatives and friends.（成貓可能把清醒時間的一半用在替自己、親屬和朋友理毛上。）注意 as much as half（多達一半）——這是一個會讓人意外的比例，作者用它來讓讀者吃驚，所以前面要配一個「信不信由你」的說法，填 Believe it or not。
誘答選項
To make matters worse（更糟的是）：要求後面接的是一件壞事，但貓花很多時間理毛在這一段裡不是壞事，整篇是在稱讚貓愛乾淨。In a nutshell（簡言之）：用來把前面講過的東西濃縮成一句，而這一句是新資訊、還在往下展開。On the other hand（另一方面）：要拿兩個相反的面向來比，本段沒有那個對照。
常考字詞與句型
Believe it or not（信不信由你）、in a nutshell（簡言之）、to make matters worse（更糟的是）、as much as（多達）、waking hours（清醒的時間）、spend time V-ing（花時間做……）。

📋 內容大意
Cats are models of cleanliness. They groom themselves with a flexible tongue, forepaws moistened by saliva, and teeth for digging out stubborn food.
Adult cats may devote half of their waking day to grooming, tending their own coats and those of relatives and friends.
Mothers lick newborn kittens to clean and comfort them. Kittens groom themselves from four weeks, then groom their mother; this shared habit is called allogrooming, which strengthens their bonds.
