Clunes, in Australia, holds a monthly book talk that hosts authors to discuss their latest 14 .
(A)trends
(B)releases
(C)agendas
(D)announcements
答案：(B) releases
題目編號：G111_14_1（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題
解析
語意「邀請作家討論他們最新的『發行作品』」，releases（發行作品，指新書）符合這個書籍主題語境；trends（趨勢）、agendas（議程）、announcements（公告）都不合。
深入解析
你可能會這樣想
release 你最熟的是動詞「釋放」（release a prisoner 釋放囚犯），所以看到名詞複數 releases 很難連到「新書」。release 當名詞指的是（新）發行的作品——新書、新專輯、新電影都算，中文常說「新作」。
怎麼判斷
整句是 Clunes, in Australia, holds a monthly book talk that hosts authors to discuss their latest ___（澳洲的 Clunes 每月舉辦書籍座談，邀請作家討論他們最新的 ___）。討論的人是 authors（作家），也就是 hosts 的受詞；修飾語是 latest（最新的）——作家會被請上台討論的「最新的東西」，在一篇通篇談書的文章裡只能是新書。
誘答選項
trends（趨勢）是市場或潮流的走向，不是作家個人的產出；agendas（議程）是會議要討論的項目清單，不是作品；announcements（公告）是對外發布的通知，作家不會在座談上討論自己最新的公告。
常考字詞與句型
a new release（新發行的作品）、release a book／an album（發行新書／新專輯）、the latest release（最新作品）、press release（新聞稿，向媒體發布的書面聲明）、host a talk（主持座談）、annual book festival（年度書展）。

A book town is a rural town in which second-hand and antiquarian bookshops are concentrated. The concept was 11 by Richard Booth, who opened the first second-hand bookshop in Hay-on-Wye, UK in 1961. Following him, many local people opened their own bookshops, and the small town soon became a model of sustainable rural development and tourism.
Since the 1970s, book towns like Hay on-Wye 12 up all over the world. Although all book towns have a great number of bookshops, that's where the similarities 13 . Each of these towns shows unique features of its own. Some have many small private shops, while others have organizations steered by volunteers. Some even run regular activities to attract visitors. For example, an annual book festival is held in Hay-on-Wye. Clunes, in Australia, holds a monthly book talk that hosts authors to discuss their latest 14 .
As digital reading is changing our traditional way of reading, book towns like Hay-on-Wye are particularly important to 15 . The feel of a book, the smell, the weight, and the knowledge that a particular book might be more than a hundred years old—all these highlight the importance of preserving the physical book as a complement to technology.
中譯：書鎮是指一個集中了許多二手書店和古書店的鄉村小鎮。這個概念最早由理查·布斯提出，他於 1961 年在英國的海伊鎮開設了第一間二手書店。在他的帶動之下，許多當地居民也紛紛開設起自己的書店，這座小鎮很快便成為永續鄉村發展與觀光的典範。自 1970 年代起，像海伊鎮這樣的書鎮已在世界各地紛紛出現。雖然所有書鎮都擁有數量眾多的書店，但它們的共通點也就只有這一點——每個書鎮都各自展現出獨特的風貌：有些擁有許多小型私人書店，有些則由志工組織營運，甚至還會定期舉辦活動來吸引遊客。舉例來說，海伊鎮每年都會舉辦書展；澳洲的克倫斯鎮則每月舉行一場書籍座談，邀請作家分享他們的新作。隨著數位閱讀逐漸改變我們傳統的閱讀方式，像海伊鎮這樣的書鎮，對於保存紙本書籍顯得格外重要。書本的觸感、氣味、重量，以及知道某本書可能已有上百年歷史的那種感受——這一切都凸顯出，在科技發達的時代，保存實體書籍作為補充的重要性。

A book town is a country town full of second-hand and antiquarian bookshops. Richard Booth started the idea in 1961 with his shop in Hay-on-Wye.
Local people then opened shops of their own. The town became a model of rural development and tourism, and similar towns have appeared worldwide since the 1970s.
The towns are also different. Some have private shops. Others are run by volunteers. Some hold festivals or author talks. They matter more now that people read on screens.
