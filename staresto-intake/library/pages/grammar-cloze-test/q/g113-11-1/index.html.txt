答對率 42%：當年每 100 位考生，有 42 位答對。
Sometimes referred to as secret shoppers, mystery shoppers 11 regular customers to purchase items, eat in restaurants, make inquiries, or return items.
(A)call for
(B)pose as
(C)attend to
(D)engage in
答案：(B) pose as
題目編號：G113_11_1（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題
解析
【句型】pose as 指「假扮成」，表示神祕客「假扮成」一般顧客去購買商品；call for（需要）、attend to（處理）、engage in（從事）都不合。
深入解析
你可能會這樣想
pose 在你腦中的第一個畫面多半是拍照時「擺姿勢」（pose for a photo），所以看到 pose as 會覺得跟句子搭不上。pose as sb 是另一個常考用法：假扮成、冒充某種身分——擺出那個身分的樣子給別人看，其實和「擺姿勢」是同一個核心概念，只是這裡擺的是身分不是姿勢。
怎麼判斷
主詞是 mystery shoppers（神祕客），同一句開頭的 Sometimes referred to as secret shoppers 已經強調「祕密」。空格後是 regular customers to purchase items（一般顧客，去買東西）——神祕客的工作正是不表明身分、裝成普通客人。再往下 They may also pretend to be ___ customers，pretend to be 就是 pose as 的同義說法，文章自己給了對照。
誘答選項
call for 是「需要、要求」（The situation calls for caution. 情況需要謹慎）；attend to 是「照料、處理」，注意 attend a meeting（出席會議）少了 to 才是「參加」，差一個 to 意思就變了；engage in 是「從事（某活動）」，後面接活動不接身分。
常考字詞與句型
pose as sb（假扮成某人）、pose a threat to sb／sth（對……構成威脅）、pose a problem for sb（為……帶來問題）、pretend to be（假裝是）、call for（需要、要求）、attend to sb（照料某人）、attend a meeting（出席會議）、engage in sth（從事……）。

Mystery shoppers are paid consumers who are hired to shop in stores and collect data. They are sent to a wide variety of service-based businesses, including stores, restaurants, and banks—potentially anywhere customers are served. Sometimes referred to as secret shoppers, mystery shoppers 11 regular customers to purchase items, eat in restaurants, make inquiries, or return items.
They grade and report on the quality of products, customer service, and environment of the stores 12 they are assigned. When evaluating a business, mystery shoppers have to follow certain standard procedures to avoid 13 . They are often given a checklist that provides directions on what to observe or look out for to ensure consistency.
Sometimes these shoppers collect data about their "normal" observations, such as cleanliness of the store or timeliness of the service. They may also pretend to be 14 customers, arguing with a salesperson without a good reason.
With the data they collect, mystery shoppers can identify areas for 15 and thereby help enhance the quality of products and services. In this way, these secret shoppers may help a business gain a competitive edge.
中譯：神祕客是受雇於店家、以消費者身分進行消費並蒐集資料的付費人員。他們被派往各式各樣提供服務的商家，包括零售店、餐廳和銀行——幾乎任何有顧客服務的地方都可能出現他們的身影。神祕客有時也被稱為秘密購物者，他們會假扮成一般顧客購買商品、在餐廳用餐、詢問問題，或辦理退貨。他們會針對所被指派商家的產品品質、顧客服務和環境進行評分並提出報告。在評鑑一間商家時，神祕客必須遵循特定的標準流程，以避免出現主觀偏差。他們通常會拿到一份檢查清單，上面列出該觀察或留意的事項，以確保評鑑結果的一致性。有時，這些神祕客會蒐集關於「日常」觀察的資料，例如商店的整潔程度或服務的即時性；他們有時也會假扮成難搞的顧客，故意無理取鬧地與店員爭執。透過蒐集到的這些資料，神祕客能找出需要改進的地方，進而協助提升產品與服務的品質。透過這樣的方式，這些秘密顧客能幫助商家在市場競爭中取得優勢。
📋 內容大意
Mystery shoppers, also called secret shoppers, are paid consumers. Businesses hire them to visit shops, restaurants and banks and gather data.
They act like ordinary customers, buying items, eating meals, asking questions or returning goods. Then they grade the products, the service and the store itself.
A checklist keeps their reports consistent and fair. They note routine things like cleanliness and service speed, and their data shows a business what to fix.
