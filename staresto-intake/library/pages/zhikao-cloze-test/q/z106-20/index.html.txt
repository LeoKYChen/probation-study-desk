In 1902 Sir Archibald Garrod was the first to attribute a 20 to an enzyme defect, which he later referred to as an "inborn error of metabolism."
(A)disease
(B)balance
(C)measure
(D)statement
答案：(A) disease
題目編號：Z106_20（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題
解析
attribute a disease to an enzyme defect 意為「把某種疾病歸因於酵素缺陷」：1902 年 Garrod 首度將疾病歸因於酵素缺陷；balance（平衡）、measure（措施）、statement（陳述）語意都不合。
深入解析
你可能會這樣想
attribute A to B（把 A 歸因於 B）這個句型你若不熟，就抓不到 A 與 B 的關係：A 是結果、B 是原因。本句 B 是 an enzyme defect（酵素缺陷），那 A 自然是它造成的後果——疾病。
怎麼判斷
同句與下一句都在確認：句中 which he later referred to as an "inborn error of metabolism"（他後來稱之為「先天代謝異常」）——error、defect 都是負面的字；下一句舉了 PKU（苯酮尿症）與 galactosemia（半乳糖血症）兩種病名為例，直接把答案講出來了。「看到專有名詞或舉例，回頭對照空格，常常一眼就確認。」attribute 的用法：attribute A to B ＝ 把 A 歸因於 B；被動是 A be attributed to B。同義說法有 owe A to B、ascribe A to B。
誘答選項
balance：平衡，是中性甚至正面的狀態，不會歸因到 defect（缺陷）上，也與後面的 error（異常）接不上。measure：措施、衡量，措施是人採取的行動，不是酵素缺陷造成的結果。statement：陳述、聲明，是人說出來的話，酵素缺陷「造成」不了陳述；下一句列的 PKU、galactosemia 也都是病名，不是陳述。
常考字詞與句型
attribute A to B（把 A 歸因於 B）、be attributed to（被歸因於）、refer to A as B（稱 A 為 B）、an enzyme defect（酵素缺陷）。

One factor that separates a living thing from an inanimate object is the organism's ability to carry out chemical reactions that are crucial for its survival. Imagine the infinite amount of reactions that a large organism such as human carries out every single day. 16 of these reactions are possible without enzymes.
Enzymes consist of various types of proteins that work to drive the chemical reactions 17 for certain types of nutrients to take effect. Enzymes can either launch a reaction or speed it up. In the absence of enzymes, reactants may take hundreds of years to convert into a usable product, if they are able to do so 18 . This is why enzymes are crucial in the sustenance of life on earth.
Enzymes, 19 , do not always function perfectly. In 1902 Sir Archibald Garrod was the first to attribute a 20 to an enzyme defect, which he later referred to as an "inborn error of metabolism." Today, newborns are routinely screened for certain enzyme defects such as PKU (phenylketonuria) and galactosemia, an error in the handling of the sugar galactose.
中譯：使生物體有別於無生命物體的其中一項因素，在於該生物體能夠進行維持其生存所必需的化學反應。試想像人類這樣的大型生物體，每天要進行的反應數量是多麼龐大。在沒有酵素的情況下，這些反應沒有一個能夠發生。酵素由多種蛋白質組成，其作用是推動某些營養素發揮效用所需的化學反應。酵素能夠啟動反應，也能加快反應速度。若缺乏酵素，反應物即使終究能夠轉化為可用的產物，也可能需要耗費數百年的時間。這正是酵素之所以對維持地球生命至關重要的原因。然而，酵素並不是每次都能完美運作。1902 年，阿奇博爾德．加羅德爵士首度將某種疾病歸因於酵素缺陷，他後來將此稱為「先天性代謝異常」。如今，新生兒會例行接受特定酵素缺陷的篩檢，例如苯酮尿症（PKU）以及一種在處理半乳糖這種糖分時出現異常的半乳糖血症。
📋 內容大意
Living things survive by carrying out countless chemical reactions, and enzymes make every one of them possible.
These proteins start reactions or hurry them along, work that would otherwise take centuries.
Enzymes can fail; such defects have been recognized since 1902, and babies are now screened for them.
