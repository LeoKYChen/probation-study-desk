110 指考閱讀測驗：餐廳的起源與發展
The Origin and Development of Restaurants
指考 110年第 40–43 題4 題
先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。
文章
第 1 段France gave birth to restaurants, but it was no civilized affair. In fact, today’s restaurant business is a byproduct of the class warfare that arose during the French Revolution.
第 2 段Back in the Middle Ages, fine dining was a privilege enjoyed exclusively by noble families who had their own grand kitchens and personal chefs. The only commercial diners for the masses were dusty, shabby roadside inns, where strangers crowded around mediocre buffets of lukewarm roasts and over-sauced beans. But sometime in the 1760s, the merchant class of Paris developed a taste for healthy clear broths which were considered restorative; hence the term “restaurant.” By the 1780s, this new Parisian “healthy food” craze led to a handful of reputable dining halls, where customers could sit at individual tables and choose from a wide range of dishes.
第 3 段Ironically, the popularity of these restaurants grew at a time when the majority of the French population could not afford bread. Decades of harsh winters and oppressive taxation had taken their toll on kitchen tables. By 1789, the starving French masses could no longer be controlled. Looting and riots erupted throughout Paris, ushering in the French Revolution. Rich nobles fled to the countryside, leaving behind their highly skilled chefs and the fine wines from their cellars. Suddenly, unemployed cooks found their way to the city’s eateries, and within a year, nearly 50 elegant restaurants had popped up in Paris.
第 4 段The restaurant business truly came into its own during the early 1800s, after General Napoleon seized control of the country. He granted “freedom of pleasure” to all citizens, as he reasoned that people who were focused on champagne and fine food probably would not conspire against him. The number of restaurants rose quickly. By 1814, about three thousand restaurants were listed in a popular travel guide. Paris became the center of the new restaurant scene, which, to some degree, it remains today.

逐段中譯
第 1 段餐廳誕生在法國，過程卻一點也不文明。事實上，今天的餐飲業，是法國大革命期間階級鬥爭的副產品。
第 2 段早在中世紀，精緻餐飲是貴族家庭獨享的特權，他們有自己的大廚房，也有私人廚師。一般大眾唯一能去的營業用餐處所，是路邊塵土滿布、破舊簡陋的旅店，陌生人擠在一起，吃的是不冷不熱的烤肉和醬汁太多的豆子，這些自助式的菜色都很普通。不過，大約在 1760 年代，巴黎的商人階級愛上了健康的清湯，大家認為這種湯能恢復元氣（restorative），「restaurant」（餐廳）這個字就是這樣來的。到了 1780 年代，這股巴黎的「健康飲食」熱潮，帶動了幾家有名的餐廳，客人可以坐在各自的桌子用餐，從許多菜色中挑選。
第 3 段諷刺的是，這些餐廳越來越受歡迎的時候，大多數法國人卻連麵包都買不起。數十年的嚴冬和沉重的苛稅，讓一般人家的餐桌越來越空。到了 1789 年，政府再也控制不了飢餓的法國群眾。巴黎各地發生搶劫和暴動，揭開了法國大革命的序幕。有錢的貴族逃到鄉下，留下了技術高超的廚師和酒窖裡的好酒。失業的廚師一下子紛紛進了城裡的餐館，不到一年，巴黎就冒出了將近 50 家高級餐廳。
第 4 段19 世紀初，拿破崙將軍掌控法國以後，餐飲業才真正蓬勃發展。他給所有公民「享樂的自由」，因為他認為，一心想著香檳和美食的人，大概不會密謀推翻他。餐廳的數量迅速增加。到了 1814 年，一本受歡迎的旅遊指南就列了大約三千家餐廳。巴黎成了這股新興餐廳風潮的中心，在某種程度上，到今天仍然是。

字詞搭配 配對與接法
「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
give birth to + N催生出……第 1 段原文：France gave birth to restaurants
a byproduct of + N……的副產品第 1 段原文：a byproduct of the class warfare
develop a taste for + N開始喜歡上……第 2 段原文：developed a taste for healthy clear broths
a handful of + N少數幾個……第 2 段原文：a handful of reputable dining halls
a wide range of + N各式各樣的……第 2 段原文：choose from a wide range of dishes
at a time when + 子句正當……的時候第 3 段原文：at a time when the majority of the French population could not afford bread
take a toll on + N對……造成損害第 3 段原文：had taken their toll on kitchen tables
usher in + N揭開……的序幕第 3 段原文：ushering in the French Revolution
find one's way to + 地方輾轉來到……第 3 段原文：unemployed cooks found their way to the city’s eateries
pop up紛紛冒出來第 3 段原文：had popped up in Paris
come into one's own發揮所長、大放異彩第 4 段原文：truly came into its own
seize control of + N奪取……的控制權第 4 段原文：seized control of the country
grant + sth + to + sb給予某人……第 4 段原文：granted “freedom of pleasure” to all citizens
conspire against + sb密謀推翻、對付某人第 4 段原文：conspire against him
