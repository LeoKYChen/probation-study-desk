115 學測閱讀測驗：螺旋梯的歷史與功能演變
The History and Functional Evolution of Spiral Staircases
學測 115年第 39–42 題4 題

文章
第 1 段In many old castles in Europe, visitors often find a fantastic spiral staircase, which provides a captivating focal point as it winds up the building. This prominent structure actually has a long and rich heritage.
第 2 段In the Old Testament, reference is made to spiral staircases in the Temple of Solomon, suggesting that they were already in use by around 1,000 BC. The oldest spiral staircase still standing today is at Trajan’s Column in Rome. The staircase was built in 113 AD, with a total of 185 steps carved in stone and marble. Around this time, spiral staircases began to find much wider use in Roman architecture and across Europe.
第 3 段Throughout the Middle Ages, winding staircases became a well-established feature of European castles, mainly for their advantages in helping to defend against attackers. To begin with, these staircases were quite narrow, so attackers would have to ascend one at a time, making it impossible to launch a mass attack. Also, the stairs were designed to turn clockwise upwards. This means that ascenders would have their right hand tight against the narrowest part of the staircases, close to the central pole, and as a result were unable to use their sword effectively. The attackers’ challenge was further complicated by the uneven steps of the staircase, often strategically designed by the castle owners. The defenders, living in the castles, were familiar with the stair pattern and could retreat up them very swiftly; while the attackers were much more likely to stumble and fall, particularly in the dimly lit confines of the staircase.
第 4 段Being associated with medieval castles and kings, spiral staircases gradually won popularity in European architecture, with new materials emerging to cope with customers’ needs. In Victorian times, cast iron spiral staircases were popular for public buildings and homes for the rich. In the latter half of the 20th century, steel frames became cost-effective, and thus affordable for a much wider staircase market. Then, steel spiral staircases as fire escape stairs appeared in many buildings. Today, spiral staircases come in a wide variety of materials: steel, wood, concrete, and recently even glass. The timeless appeal of their classical design makes spiral staircases a much-desired feature in luxury homes, offices, and public buildings nowadays.

逐段中譯
第 1 段在歐洲許多古堡裡，遊客常會看到一座漂亮的螺旋梯沿著建築盤旋而上，成為吸引目光的焦點。這種顯眼的結構，其實有一段悠久而豐富的歷史。
第 2 段舊約聖經提到所羅門聖殿裡有螺旋梯，可見大約西元前 1000 年就已經有人使用。現存最古老、至今仍然屹立的螺旋梯，在羅馬的圖拉真柱裡面。這座螺旋梯建於西元 113 年，共有 185 級，是用石頭和大理石鑿成的。大約從這個時候起，螺旋梯在羅馬建築、甚至整個歐洲都用得越來越廣。
第 3 段整個中世紀，螺旋梯成了歐洲城堡常見的設計，主要是因為它有助於抵禦敵人。首先，這種樓梯相當狹窄，進攻的人只能一個接一個往上爬，沒辦法大舉進攻。其次，樓梯設計成順時針往上轉，也就是說，往上爬的人右手會緊貼著樓梯最窄、靠近中柱的那一側，因此沒辦法好好揮劍。城堡主人往往還刻意把階梯做得高低不一，讓進攻的一方更加吃力。住在城堡裡的守方熟悉階梯的樣子，可以很快退到樓上；進攻的人在昏暗狹小的樓梯間裡，就容易絆倒摔跤。
第 4 段螺旋梯讓人聯想到中世紀的城堡和國王，在歐洲建築裡漸漸流行起來，也陸續出現新的材料來因應顧客的需求。維多利亞時期，鑄鐵螺旋梯常見於公共建築和富人的住宅。到了二十世紀下半葉，鋼骨架變得划算，更多人買得起，螺旋梯的市場也跟著擴大。後來，許多建築開始用鋼製螺旋梯當逃生梯。如今螺旋梯的材質五花八門，有鋼、木頭、混凝土，最近甚至還有玻璃。古典的設計歷久不衰，讓螺旋梯成為現今豪宅、辦公室和公共建築很受青睞的設計。

字詞搭配 配對與接法
「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
