Taiwu Elementary School Folk Singers

文章
第 1 段In 2009, the Taiwu Elementary School Folk Singers were invited to perform in Belgium, France, Germany, and Luxemburg. In 2011, they were voted as one of the world’s top five performance groups by audiences of Japan Broadcasting Corporation’s Amazing Voice program.
第 2 段Recalling the group’s first tour in Europe, Camake Valaule, a physical education teacher and the founder of the Taiwu Elementary School Folk Singers, admitted that he felt very nervous. He was worried that the audience would fall asleep since most of the 75-minute performance was a cappella, that is, singing without instrumental sound. Surprisingly, the audience listened with full focus and high spirits. Camake said, “They told me afterward that through our performance, they had a vision of our country, our village, without having to visit it. This experience greatly boosted our confidence.”
第 3 段According to Camake Valaule, singing traditional ballads has helped students and their parents to re-understand their culture. “It used to be that the only ones who could sing these songs were tribal elders aged between 50 and 60. Now with the children performing the pieces, parents are beginning to ask, ‘Why do we not know how to sing these ballads?’ Many times nowadays, it is the children who teach the songs to their parents, putting back the pieces of a blurred memory.”
第 4 段Winning international fame, however, was neither the original intention nor the main reason why Camake founded the group in 2006. The most important thing was to make children understand why they sing these songs and to preserve and pass on their culture. Referring to the relocation of Taiwu Elementary School and Taiwu Village following Typhoon Morakot in August 2009, Camake said, “We could not take the forest or our houses in the mountains with us; but we were able to bring our culture along. As long as the children are willing to sing, I will always be there for them, singing with them and leading them to experience the meaning of the ballads.”

逐段中譯
第 1 段2009 年，泰武國小古謠傳唱隊受邀到比利時、法國、德國和盧森堡演出。2011 年，傳唱隊獲日本放送協會（NHK）《Amazing Voice》節目的觀眾票選為全球五大表演團體之一。
第 2 段體育老師 Camake Valaule 是泰武國小古謠傳唱隊的創辦人，回想起傳唱隊第一次到歐洲巡迴演出，他坦承當時非常緊張。75 分鐘的演出大部分是 a cappella，也就是沒有樂器伴奏的清唱，所以他擔心觀眾會睡著。出乎意料的是，觀眾聽得全神貫注、興致高昂。Camake 說：「他們事後告訴我，透過我們的演出，他們不必親自造訪，就看見了我們的國家、我們的村子。這次經驗讓我們的信心大增。」
第 3 段Camake Valaule 說，唱傳統古謠讓學生和家長重新認識自己的文化。「以前會唱這些歌的，只有五、六十歲的部落長老。現在孩子們唱這些歌，家長開始問：『為什麼我們不會唱這些古謠？』如今常常是孩子教父母唱，把模糊的記憶一片一片拼回來。」
第 4 段不過，Camake 在 2006 年成立傳唱隊，原本的用意並不是贏得國際名聲，這也不是主要的理由。最重要的是讓孩子明白自己為什麼唱這些歌，並且保存、傳承自己的文化。談到 2009 年 8 月莫拉克颱風過後泰武國小和泰武村遷校遷村，Camake 說：「我們帶不走山上的森林和房子，但是我們可以把文化帶著走。只要孩子們願意唱，我就會一直陪著他們，和他們一起唱，帶他們體會古謠的意義。」

字詞搭配 配對與接法
「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
be voted (as) + N獲選為……第 1 段原文：they were voted as one of the world’s top five performance groups
with full focus全神貫注第 2 段原文：the audience listened with full focus and high spirits
boost one's confidence增加信心第 2 段原文：This experience greatly boosted our confidence
win international fame揚名國際第 4 段原文：Winning international fame
pass on + N傳承……第 4 段原文：to preserve and pass on their culture
refer to + N提到……第 4 段原文：Referring to the relocation of Taiwu Elementary School
be willing to + V願意……第 4 段原文：As long as the children are willing to sing
be there for + sb陪伴、支持某人第 4 段原文：I will always be there for them
