The True Origin of Monopoly

文章
第 1 段Monopoly is a very popular board game around the world today, but little is known about its American inventor, Elizabeth Magie, and the philosophy behind her invention.
第 2 段Born in 1866, Magie was a rebel against the norms and politics of her times. Inspired by Henry George who believed that all men should have an equal right to use the land as they have to breathe air, she challenged the capitalist system of property ownership—in the form of a board game. In 1904, she patented her Landlord’s Game, which consisted of a circuit of streets and landmarks for sale.
第 3 段Magie’s game contained two sets of rules: The Prosperity rules and the Monopolist rules. The Prosperity rules stated that every player should gain each time someone acquired a new property. The game was won (by all!) when the player starting with the least money doubled his or her fortune. Under Monopolist rules, on the other hand, each player advanced by acquiring properties and collecting rent from all who landed there later. Whoever managed to bankrupt the other players won the game. The purpose of the dual sets of rules, said Magie, was for players to understand how these different approaches to property can lead to different social outcomes: “all win” or “win all.”
第 4 段The game soon became a hit on college campuses and among Quaker communities, and some people modified the game board. An unemployed player named Charles Darrow sold this modified version to the manufacturer Parker Brothers as his own. However, when the game’s true origin came to light, Parker Brothers bought the patent from Magie for only $500. They then re-launched the game as Monopoly, including only the rules leading to the triumph of one over all. Darrow was publicized as the inventor who had become a millionaire from selling the game. Thus a rags-to-riches myth was created, ironically exemplifying Monopoly’s implicit values: Chase wealth and crush your opponents if you want to come out on top.

逐段中譯
第 1 段大富翁是今天風行全世界的桌遊，但很少人知道它的美國發明人 Elizabeth Magie，以及她發明這款遊戲背後的理念。
第 2 段Magie 生於 1866 年，是個反抗當時社會規範和政治的人。Henry George 認為，人人都應該有平等使用土地的權利，就像人人都有呼吸空氣的權利一樣；Magie 從他的理念得到啟發，用桌遊的形式挑戰資本主義的土地財產所有制。1904 年，她為自己的「地主遊戲」申請專利，遊戲由一圈可以買賣的街道和地標組成。
第 3 段Magie 的遊戲有兩套規則：繁榮規則和壟斷規則。繁榮規則規定，每當有人取得新的地產，每一位玩家都能獲益；等到起始資金最少的玩家把財富翻倍，遊戲就算贏了，而且是所有人一起贏！壟斷規則則不同，每位玩家靠取得地產、向之後停在那裡的人收租來前進，能讓其他玩家全部破產的人就是贏家。Magie 說，設計兩套規則的用意，是讓玩家了解對待財產的不同方式，會帶來不同的社會結果：「大家都贏」或「贏家通吃」。
第 4 段這款遊戲很快在大學校園和貴格會社群中流行起來，有些人還修改了遊戲版面。一個名叫 Charles Darrow 的失業玩家，把這個修改版當成自己的作品，賣給遊戲廠商 Parker Brothers。不過，遊戲的真正起源曝光之後，Parker Brothers 只花 500 美元就向 Magie 買下專利，接著把遊戲改名為大富翁重新推出，只保留讓一個人打敗所有人的規則。Darrow 則在宣傳中成了靠賣遊戲成為百萬富翁的發明人。一段白手起家的神話就這樣誕生，諷刺的是，它正好體現了大富翁隱含的價值觀：想要出人頭地，就要追求財富、擊垮對手。

字詞搭配 配對與接法
「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
little is known about + N對……所知甚少第 1 段原文：little is known about its American inventor
a rebel against + N反抗……的人第 2 段原文：a rebel against the norms and politics of her times
have an equal right to + V有同等的權利……第 2 段原文：have an equal right to use the land
in the form of + N以……的形式第 2 段原文：in the form of a board game
consist of + N由……組成第 2 段原文：consisted of a circuit of streets and landmarks
for sale出售的第 2 段原文：landmarks for sale
collect rent收租第 3 段原文：collecting rent from all who landed there later
manage to + V設法做到……第 3 段原文：Whoever managed to bankrupt the other players
