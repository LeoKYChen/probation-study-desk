97 學測閱讀測驗：冰河國家公園道路的建設與公園合作
Glacier National Park Road Construction and Park Collaboration
學測 97年第 45–48 題4 題
先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。
文章
第 1 段After the creation of the Glacier National Park in Montana, the growing number of park visitors increased the need for roads. Eventually, the demand for a road across the mountains led to the building of the Going-to-the-Sun Road.
第 2 段The construction of the Going-to-the-Sun Road was a huge task. After 11 years of work, the final section of the road was completed in 1932. The road is considered an engineering feat. Even today, visitors to the park marvel at how such a road could have been built. It is one of the most scenic roads in North America. The construction of the road has changed the way visitors experience the Glacier National Park. Visitors now can drive over sections of the park that previously took days of horseback riding to see.
第 3 段Just across the border, in Canada, is the Waterton Lakes National Park. In 1931, members of the Rotary Clubs of Alberta and Montana suggested joining the two parks as a symbol of peace and friendship between the two countries. In 1932, the United States and Canadian governments renamed the parks the Waterton-Glacier International Peace Park, the world’s first. More recently, the parks have received several international honors. They were named as a World Heritage Site in 1995. This international recognition highlights the importance of this area, not just to the United States and Canada, but to the entire world.
題目
45.What made it necessary to build a road through the Glacier National Park?
(A)There were too many parks in Montana.
(B)The park was not sunny enough for visitors.
(C)The existing mountain roads were destroyed.
(D)More visitors were interested in going to the park.
46.How has the Going-to-the-Sun Road influenced the way people experience the Glacier National Park?
(A)The scenery along the road is too beautiful for visitors to drive carefully.
(B)It has become a marvelous experience for people to ride horses on this road.
(C)The road has allowed people to see more of the park in a shorter period of time.
(D)The transportation on the road was so difficult that few people could really enjoy the trip.
47.What does “an engineering feat” mean?
(A)A big success in construction.
(B)A magical building machine.
(C)A great disaster for the travelers.
(D)An enjoyable process for engineers.
48.What is special about the Waterton-Glacier International Peace Park?
(A)It is where the glacier runs to the lake.
(B)It is the first park funded by the whole world.
(C)It is a special park built to protect wild animals.
(D)It is composed of two parks located in two countries.

第 45 題 細節題 Supporting details
第 1 段After the creation of the Glacier National Park in Montana, the growing number of park visitors increased the need for roads.
原文 → 正確選項
growing number of park visitors → More visitors
第 1 段說冰河國家公園成立以後，遊客越來越多（the growing number of park visitors），對道路的需求跟著增加，選項換成 More visitors were interested in going to the park，所以是 (D)。(A) 照抄 Montana，蒙大拿州是公園所在的州，原文沒說那裡的公園太多；(B) 照抄 Sun，奔向太陽之路是道路的名字，和公園陽光夠不夠無關；(C) 照抄 mountain，原文說需要一條翻越山脈的路，沒說原有的山路毀了。
第 46 題 細節題 Supporting details
第 2 段The construction of the road has changed the way visitors experience the Glacier National Park.
第 2 段Visitors now can drive over sections of the park that previously took days of horseback riding to see.
第 2 段說這條路改變了遊客體驗公園的方式：以前要騎好幾天馬才看得到的地方，現在開車就能經過。從好幾天縮短到開車就到，遊客能在比較短的時間裡看到公園更多地方，所以是 (C)。(B) 照抄 horse，騎馬是以前的方式，有了這條路以後改成開車；(A) 照抄 drive，原文說這是北美洲風景最美的道路之一，沒說風景美到讓人沒辦法小心開車；(D) 正好相反，原文說遊客開車就能經過那些地方。

第 48 題 細節題 Supporting details
第 3 段In 1931, members of the Rotary Clubs of Alberta and Montana suggested joining the two parks as a symbol of peace and friendship between the two countries.
原文 → 正確選項
two parks → two parks
two countries → two countries
第 3 段說加拿大那一邊有華特頓湖國家公園，扶輪社成員提議把兩座公園（two parks）結合起來，象徵兩國（two countries）的和平與友誼；1932 年兩國政府把它們命名為國際和平公園。選項換成 composed of two parks located in two countries，所以是 (D)。(B) 照抄 first，原文說它是世界第一座國際和平公園，後來列為世界遺產，沒說由全世界出資；(A) 照抄 lake，華特頓湖是加拿大那座公園的名字，原文沒說冰河流進湖裡；(C) 原文沒說這座公園是為了保護野生動物而建的。
逐段中譯
第 1 段蒙大拿州的冰河國家公園成立以後，遊客越來越多，對道路的需求也增加了。後來大家需要一條翻越山脈的道路，促成了「奔向太陽之路」的興建。
第 2 段興建奔向太陽之路是一項浩大的工程。經過 11 年的施工，最後一段路在 1932 年完工。這條路公認是一項工程壯舉。直到今天，來公園的遊客仍然驚嘆這樣一條路是怎麼蓋起來的。這是北美洲風景最優美的道路之一。這條路改變了遊客體驗冰河國家公園的方式：公園裡有些地方，以前要騎好幾天的馬才看得到，現在遊客開車就能經過。
第 3 段越過邊界，加拿大那一邊是華特頓湖國家公園。1931 年，亞伯達省和蒙大拿州的扶輪社成員提議把兩座公園結合起來，象徵兩國之間的和平與友誼。1932 年，美國和加拿大政府把這兩座公園重新命名為華特頓冰河國際和平公園，這是世界上第一座國際和平公園。近年來，這兩座公園獲得了幾項國際榮譽，1995 年列為世界遺產。這項國際肯定凸顯出這個地區不只對美國和加拿大重要，對全世界都很重要。

字詞搭配 配對與接法
「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
lead to + N導致……第 1 段原文：the demand for a road across the mountains led to the building of the Going-to-the-Sun Road
an engineering feat工程上的壯舉第 2 段原文：The road is considered an engineering feat
marvel at + N對……感到驚嘆第 2 段原文：visitors to the park marvel at how such a road could have been built
change the way + 子句改變……的方式第 2 段原文：has changed the way visitors experience the Glacier National Park
