108 學測閱讀測驗：神仙魚的變性能力
The Gender-Changing Angelfish
學測 108年第 41–44 題4 題
先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。
文章
第 1 段Angelfish, often found in the warm seas and coral reefs, are among the most brightly colored fish of the ocean. Brilliant colors and stripes form amazing patterns on their body. These patterns actually help the fish to hide from danger among roots and plants. At night, when these fish become inactive, their colors may become pale. Often, the young ones are differently colored than the adults. Some scientists believe that the color difference between the young and the old indicates their different social positions.
第 2 段Another interesting fact about angelfish is that they have an occupation in the fish world. Most of them act as cleaners for other fish and pick dead tissue from their bodies. This is not their food, though. Their diet consists mainly of sponge and algae.
第 3 段One particular kind of angelfish, the blackspot angelfish, has a special capability that allows it to change gender from female to male. However, the change is not made at random; it happens for a specific reason. Angelfish live in groups, and each group has one male fish, which is blue in color, and four female fish, which are yellow. The male angelfish is the strongest and largest member of the group. He is the one who protects and looks after the females. When the male dies, the group needs a new “security guard.” This is when the largest female in the group begins to change in appearance. She begins to grow larger in size, and after a week, she starts changing color, from yellow to blue. Slowly, her behavior toward the other fish also changes. She begins behaving like a male. Two weeks later, black stripes appear on her body, indicating the gender change is complete. She is now completely male!
題目
41.What is the job of an angelfish in the sea?
(A)Being a cleaner for other fish.
(B)Being a bodyguard for other fish.
(C)Being a gardener for roots and plants.
(D)Being a caretaker for sponge and algae.
42.Which of the following statements is true about the color of angelfish?
(A)Female angelfish are blue in color.
(B)The colors of the fish become less bright at night.
(C)Male angelfish do not have black stripes on them.
(D)The adult fish and the young ones have the same colors.
43.According to the passage, what triggers the gender change in the blackspot angelfish?
(A)Dangers to the group.
(B)Changes in the fish’s diet.
(C)The birth of young angelfish.
(D)The death of the male fish in a group.
44.Which of the following describes the order of changes in the features of a blackspot angelfish during gender change?
(A)Size → behavior → color → stripes.
(B)Color → size → behavior → stripes.
(C)Size → color → behavior → stripes.
(D)Color → behavior → size → stripes.

第 1 段At night, when these fish become inactive, their colors may become pale.

第 44 題 細節題 Supporting details
第 3 段She begins to grow larger in size, and after a week, she starts changing color, from yellow to blue.
第 3 段Slowly, her behavior toward the other fish also changes.
第 3 段Two weeks later, black stripes appear on her body, indicating the gender change is complete.
原文 → 正確選項
larger in size → Size
changing color → color
her behavior → behavior
black stripes → stripes
第 3 段依序寫出四個變化：先是體型變大（size），一週後變色（color），接著對其他魚的行為慢慢改變（behavior），兩週後出現黑色條紋（stripes）。after a week、Slowly、Two weeks later 這幾個表示先後的字詞標出了順序，所以是 (C)。(A) 把行為排在變色之前，可是變色是一週後，行為的改變在變色之後才慢慢出現；(B)、(D) 都從變色開始，可是原文先寫體型變大，一週後才變色，(D) 還把體型排到行為之後。
逐段中譯
第 1 段神仙魚常見於溫暖的海域和珊瑚礁，是海洋中色彩最鮮豔的魚類之一。亮麗的色彩和條紋在牠們身上形成令人驚嘆的花紋，這些花紋其實能幫助牠們躲在樹根和植物之間，避開危險。到了晚上，神仙魚不太活動，顏色可能會變淡。幼魚的顏色也常常和成魚不同，有些科學家認為，這種顏色上的差異代表牠們在群體中的地位不同。
第 2 段神仙魚另一個有趣的地方，是牠們在魚類世界裡有一份「職業」。大部分的神仙魚替其他魚當清潔工，啄掉牠們身上的死皮。不過，這些死皮並不是牠們的食物，牠們主要吃的是海綿和藻類。
第 3 段有一種神仙魚叫黑斑神仙魚，具有一種特殊的能力，可以從雌性變成雄性。不過，這種轉變不是隨機發生的，而是有特定的原因。神仙魚成群生活，每一群有一條藍色的雄魚和四條黃色的雌魚。雄魚是群體裡最強壯、體型最大的成員，負責保護和照顧雌魚。雄魚死掉之後，群體需要一位新的「守衛」，這時群體裡最大的雌魚就開始改變外觀。牠先是體型開始變大，一週之後，顏色從黃色變成藍色。接著，牠對其他魚的行為也慢慢改變，開始表現得像雄魚。兩週之後，牠身上出現黑色條紋，表示變性已經完成。牠現在完全是一條雄魚了！

字詞搭配 配對與接法
「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
