112 學測閱讀測驗：大象與茶園的衝突
Elephants and Tea Gardens
學測 112年第 39–42 題4 題
先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。
文章
第 1 段When you enjoy your morning cup of tea, you are probably not aware that those tea leaves can mean injury, or even death, for Asian elephants roaming Indian tea gardens.
第 2 段In the Indian state of Assam, growing numbers of tea farms are destroying the Asian elephant’s habitats and endangering their population. Much of the forest land where tea is grown in Assam is flat and thus farmers must dig drainage trenches to prevent water from accumulating and hurting the shrubs. The trenches, however, can be death traps for the elephants. Since the elephants need to use tea plantations as landmarks when navigating forests, they almost inevitably have to move through the farms. Moreover, because there are fewer humans around, pregnant females often use tea-growing areas as safe shelters to give birth. But baby elephants, not used to negotiating rough ground, may easily fall into the trenches and get hurt; and once injured, they might not be able to climb out. When mothers try to dig their babies out, both may be trapped and smothered by thick mud. Furthermore, elephants are known to resist leaving their sick or dying behind, and a herd may linger at a trench with a trapped baby for hours, reluctant to move on until all hope is lost.
第 3 段Is it possible for elephants to coexist with the prosperous tea business? Elephant Friendly Tea is an organization that takes the initiative to make it possible. The organization encourages consumers to choose brands that take precautions to protect elephants and has set up a certification program to reward tea growers who are doing it right. Until now, only smaller tea brands have been certified, but awareness is growing. The organization believes that people may be motivated to buy elephant-friendly brands when they know more about the risk tea can pose to these endangered animals.
題目
39.Why do farmers in Assam dig trenches in tea gardens?
(A)To protect tea trees.
(B)To trap elephants.
(C)To expand tea farms.
(D)To mark boundaries of tea gardens.
40.Why are baby elephants easily injured in the Assam tea gardens according to the passage?
(A)They cannot find a safe shelter when climbing out of the trenches.
(B)They cannot locate the landmarks while trying to navigate forests.
(C)They are trapped by the sharp branches of the tea trees.
(D)They have difficulties moving around the uneven fields.
41.Which of the following statements about elephants and the tea gardens is true according to the passage?
(A)The elephants use the trenches to roam around the tea gardens.
(B)The fast growth of the tea gardens destroys the elephants’ food source.
(C)Elephants are unwilling to leave their injured members behind in the tea gardens.
(D)Pregnant elephants avoid delivering babies in the tea gardens for fear of being disturbed.
42.What does “it” in the last paragraph refer to?
(A)To certify elephant-friendly trenches and organizations.
(B)To reward tea growers for protecting the environment.
(C)To encourage consumers to choose high-quality brands.
(D)To create a win-win situation for elephants and tea farms.

每一題的依據句
第 39 題 細節題 Supporting details
第 2 段Much of the forest land where tea is grown in Assam is flat and thus farmers must dig drainage trenches to prevent water from accumulating and hurting the shrubs.
原文 → 正確選項
to prevent water from accumulating and hurting the shrubs → To protect tea trees
挖排水溝是為了不讓積水傷害茶樹，原文的 shrubs 在選項換成 tea trees。(B) 照抄下一句的 trap，但溝渠變成大象的死亡陷阱是意外，不是挖溝的目的。(C) 第 2 段開頭說茶園愈來愈多，那是茶園擴張破壞大象的棲地，挖溝並不能擴大茶園；(D) 的 mark 呼應同一段的 landmarks，可是拿茶園當地標的是大象，溝渠也不是用來標出茶園的邊界。
第 40 題 細節題 Supporting details
第 2 段But baby elephants, not used to negotiating rough ground, may easily fall into the trenches and get hurt
原文 → 正確選項
not used to → have difficulties
negotiating rough ground → moving around the uneven fields
negotiate 在這裡是「通過、走過」，不是談判；小象不習慣走崎嶇的地面，在選項換成在不平的田裡行動有困難，rough 換成 uneven。(A) 照抄 safe shelter 和 climb out，但安全的庇護所是母象選來生產的地方；(B) 照抄 landmarks，那是成年大象辨認方向用的。(C) 文章沒有提到茶樹的枝條，小象受傷是因為掉進溝渠。
第 41 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details
第 2 段Furthermore, elephants are known to resist leaving their sick or dying behind, and a herd may linger at a trench with a trapped baby for hours, reluctant to move on until all hope is lost.
原文 → 正確選項
resist leaving their sick or dying behind → unwilling to leave their injured members behind
resist leaving 換成 unwilling to leave，sick or dying 換成 injured members。(D) 照抄 Pregnant，但原文正好相反，母象常到茶園生產；(A) 照抄 trenches，溝渠是陷阱，不是大象移動的路線；(B) 文章說茶園破壞的是棲地，沒說食物來源。
第 42 題 詞義題（指代詞） Vocabulary in context
第 3 段Is it possible for elephants to coexist with the prosperous tea business?
第 3 段Elephant Friendly Tea is an organization that takes the initiative to make it possible.
原文 → 正確選項
elephants to coexist with the prosperous tea business → a win-win situation for elephants and tea farms
make it possible 回應前一句的問句 Is it possible …：讓大象和茶葉產業共存，也就是兩全其美。(A) 照抄同一段的 certification，可是第 3 段說認證的對象是做好保護措施的茶農和茶葉品牌，不是溝渠，也不是組織；(B)、(C) 照抄後一句組織的做法，那是達成目標的方法，不是 it 本身；(C) 的 high-quality 原文也沒提。

逐段中譯
第 1 段當你享用早晨那杯茶時，大概不知道那些茶葉對漫步在印度茶園裡的亞洲象來說，可能意味著受傷，甚至死亡。
第 2 段在印度的阿薩姆邦，茶園越來越多，正在破壞亞洲象的棲地，危及亞洲象的生存。阿薩姆種茶的林地大多平坦，農民必須挖排水溝，免得積水傷害茶樹。然而這些溝渠可能成為大象的死亡陷阱。大象在森林裡辨認方向時，需要拿茶園當地標，所以幾乎不可避免地要穿過茶園。此外，由於茶園附近人比較少，懷孕的母象常把種茶的地方當作安全的庇護所來生產。可是小象還不習慣走崎嶇不平的地面，很容易掉進溝裡受傷；一旦受傷，小象可能就爬不出來。母象想把小象挖出來時，母子可能都會陷在厚厚的泥巴裡窒息。再者，大象向來不肯丟下生病或垂死的同伴，整個象群可能守在困住小象的溝渠旁好幾個小時，直到完全絕望才肯離開。
第 3 段大象有可能和興旺的茶葉產業共存嗎？「大象友善茶」這個組織主動出擊，想讓這件事成真。這個組織鼓勵消費者選擇有採取措施保護大象的品牌，還設立了認證制度，獎勵做法正確的茶農。到目前為止，取得認證的只有規模較小的茶葉品牌，但大家越來越關心這個問題。這個組織相信，人們更了解茶葉可能對這些瀕危動物造成的危險，就會更願意購買對大象友善的品牌。

綱要
喝茶時很少人知道，茶葉可能讓印度茶園裡的亞洲象受傷甚至死亡。
阿薩姆茶園擴張破壞象群棲地，農民挖排水溝保護茶樹，(however) 溝渠卻成了死亡陷阱。
(Since) 大象穿越茶園認路，(Moreover) 母象也在茶園生產，(But) 小象不熟悉崎嶇地面，容易跌落受傷，母象救援時也可能一起受困。
(Furthermore) 大象不願丟下受困的同伴，會在溝邊守好幾個小時。
「大象友善茶」組織設立認證，鼓勵消費者選購，(Until now) 目前認證的是小品牌，但意識逐漸提高。

字詞搭配 配對與接法
「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
be aware that + 子句知道……第 1 段原文：you are probably not aware that
prevent + N + from + V-ing防止……第 2 段原文：to prevent water from accumulating
a death trap死亡陷阱第 2 段原文：can be death traps for the elephants
give birth生產第 2 段
be used to + V-ing習慣……第 2 段原文：not used to negotiating rough ground
resist + V-ing不肯……第 2 段原文：resist leaving their sick or dying behind
leave + sb + behind丟下某人第 2 段原文：resist leaving their sick or dying behind
be reluctant to + V不願意……第 2 段原文：reluctant to move on
coexist with + N和……共存第 3 段
take the initiative主動第 3 段原文：takes the initiative to make it possible
take precautions採取預防措施第 3 段原文：take precautions to protect elephants
be motivated to + V有動機去……第 3 段原文：may be motivated to buy elephant-friendly brands
pose a risk to + N對……構成危險第 3 段原文：the risk tea can pose to these endangered animals
