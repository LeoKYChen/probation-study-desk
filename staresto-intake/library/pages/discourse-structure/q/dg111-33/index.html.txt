學測 111年 篇章結構 第33題
選文：日本盂蘭盆節（The Obon Festival）
掌握篇章整體結構發展及段落與段落間的關係線索：One… Another… Some… Others 句型答對率 47%
(A)Some people also visit the cemetery to clean up the family graves and pray for their ancestors.有些人也會去墓園整理家族墓地、向祖先祭拜。
(B)Such festive activities are usually held in parks, temples, and other public places around Japan.這類節慶活動通常在日本各地的公園、寺廟和其他公共場所舉行。
(C)Obon concludes with another bonfire, okuribi, lighting up the sky to see the ancestors’ spirits off.Obon 以另一場火 okuribi 作結，照亮天空，送走祖先的靈魂。
(D)Originating from the Chinese Ghost Festival, this annual event has evolved into a time of family reunion.這個一年一度的節日源自中國的中元節，如今已演變成家人團聚的時節。
答案：(C) Obon concludes with another bonfire, okuribi, lighting up the sky to see the ancestors’ spirits off.
題目編號：Dg111_33
解析
先看空格的位置。 它在第三段的最前面，整段的第一句。段首的空格通常要交代「這一段要講什麼」。
再看空格後面。 後面說紙燈籠和供品被放進河裡漂走，「陪祖先回到安息之處」。關鍵在 back（回去）——第二段講的是把靈魂迎回來，這裡講的是送回去。
C 的 concludes（作結）與 see...off（送別）正好是這一段的分工，another bonfire（另一場火）也回應第二段的迎火。
如果你選了 B（這類節慶活動通常在日本各地的公園、寺廟和其他公共場所舉行。）：B 的 Such（這類）必須指回前面提過的東西，可是段首之前是上一段的結尾（用小黃瓜做馬、茄子做牛），指不過去。
【下次先看這裡】空格在段落最前面，先讀完整段再回頭選——段首那一句要能罩住整段。

Obon, or the Bon Festival, is a Japanese holiday that honors the spirits of the dead. 31 The festival usually lasts for four to five days in August. During this period, many people travel back to their hometowns and spend time with loved ones, both past and present.
Though not a national holiday, Obon is surely one of the most traditional events of the year. Celebration often begins with mukaebi (welcoming fire), during which people make a small bonfire in front of their house to guide spirits upon their return back home. 32 Food offerings are presented at house altars and temples. Some regions prepare horses made of cucumbers and cows made of eggplants, hoping that the spirits will come back to Earth quickly, on a horse, and leave slowly, riding the cow.
33Obon concludes with another bonfire, okuribi, lighting up the sky to see the ancestors’ spirits off. Paper lanterns and offerings are sent floating down rivers to accompany the ancestors back to their resting place. Many areas will also organize bon-odori dances. The style of the dances varies from region to region but is normally based on the rhythms of taiko drums. Performers usually play on a tall stage with lanterns and banners strung all around. Participants, often dressed in light cotton kimonos, are encouraged to dance to the music around the stage. 34 Originally dedicated to the deceased, the dances have now become a symbol of the summer festival themselves.
中譯
Obon，即盂蘭盆節，是日本一個追念亡者靈魂的節日。31 這個節日通常在八月，為期四到五天。這段期間，許多人會回到故鄉，和所愛的人共度時光——包括已經過世的和還在世的。
盂蘭盆節雖然不是國定假日，卻無疑是一年之中最傳統的活動之一。慶祝通常從 mukaebi（迎火）開始，人們在自家門前生一小堆火，替返家的靈魂引路。32 供品會擺在家中的佛壇和寺廟裡。有些地區會用小黃瓜做馬、用茄子做牛，希望靈魂騎馬快快回到人間，再騎牛慢慢離開。
33 紙燈籠和供品會被放進河裡漂流，陪伴祖先回到安息之處。許多地方也會舉辦 bon-odori（盆舞）。舞蹈的形式各地不同，但通常以太鼓的節奏為基礎。表演者多半在一座高台上演出，四周掛滿燈籠和旗幟。參加的人常穿著輕薄的棉質浴衣，可以隨著音樂繞著高台起舞。34 這些舞蹈原本是為亡者而跳，如今本身已成為夏日祭典的象徵。
